# Transfer Security Playbook

The purchase isn't complete when you pay — it's complete when the account is
exclusively yours. This playbook expands Steps 17–19 of the main guide into
a checklist you can follow line by line.

---

## Phase 1: Credentials (do this first, in order)

- [ ] **Change the password.** Long, unique, generated by a password
manager. Never reuse a password from another service.
- [ ] **Verify the attached email.** Confirm you control the email address
handed over with the account.
- [ ] **Add your personal email** as the primary address on the GitHub
account.
- [ ] **Remove any email addresses** you don't recognize or control.

## Phase 2: Recovery (do this second)

- [ ] **Enable two-factor authentication.** Use an authenticator app rather
than SMS where possible.
- [ ] **Generate fresh recovery codes.** Store them offline — printed or in
a secure vault, not in a notes app.
- [ ] **Confirm the old recovery methods are gone.** No lingering phone
numbers or backup emails you don't own.

## Phase 3: Access audit (do this third)

- [ ] **Review active sessions.** Revoke every session you don't recognize —
when in doubt, revoke it and log back in.
- [ ] **Remove old SSH keys and deploy keys.** If you didn't add it, delete
it.
- [ ] **Check authorized OAuth applications.** Disconnect anything unfamiliar
or unnecessary.
- [ ] **Review personal access tokens.** Revoke any you didn't create.

## Phase 4: Ongoing hygiene

- [ ] **Use a unique password** for this account, forever. No sharing across
services.
- [ ] **Keep 2FA on.** If you ever disable it temporarily, re-enable it the
same day.
- [ ] **Glance at the security log** occasionally for logins you don't
recognize.

---

## Why this matters

Every sale here is a **full ownership transfer**, not a shared login. The
platform strips old sessions and rotates credentials before listing, and
only sells accounts it fully controls — so there's no previous owner who
could attempt a recovery. But the final mile is yours: until you've run this
playbook, the transfer isn't finished.

If anything goes wrong during or after the transfer, the **replacement
warranty** covers access issues within the warranty period — free replacement
with an equivalent or better profile — and support is reachable 24/7 via
Telegram and WhatsApp.
