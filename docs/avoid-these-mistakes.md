# 7 Mistakes Buyers Make (And How to Dodge Them)

## 1. Buying before defining the goal

The classic error: purchasing a Legend-tier profile for a bot that just
needed a login — or buying Fresh when clients will inspect the profile.
Write down your goal first (see Step 1 of the guide). Everything else
follows from it.

## 2. Ignoring the badge timeline

A listing claims Arctic Code Vault on an account created in 2022? Impossible
— the snapshot was 2020. Time-locked badges must line up with the account's
age. This is the single easiest fraud check you can run, and too many buyers
skip it.

## 3. Skipping the contribution-graph inspection

Don't just glance at the green squares — read them. Real history has rhythm:
sprints and lulls, varied hours, multiple repos. A flat, mechanical pattern
deserves skepticism no matter what the listing says.

## 4. Paying with the wrong expectations about delivery

Standard delivery is minutes via email and Telegram. Premium tiers include a
manual quality check, so allow same-day rather than same-minute. Neither is
slow — but knowing which to expect avoids unnecessary panic.

## 5. Reusing the handover password

The credentials you receive are transit credentials. They did their job the
moment you logged in. Change the password immediately (Step 17) — running on
handover credentials is the digital equivalent of never changing the locks.

## 6. Forgetting 2FA and recovery codes

Password changed but no two-factor authentication? You've done half the job.
Enable 2FA, generate fresh recovery codes, and store them offline. An account
without 2FA is an account you're renting from luck.

## 7. Leaving old sessions, keys, and apps behind

The transfer checklist exists for a reason: active sessions, SSH keys, and
authorized OAuth apps are all doors into the account. Audit every one of
them in Step 19. A locked front door means nothing with the windows open.
