# Quick FAQ

## What am I actually buying?

An established GitHub account with genuine history — contributions, merged
pull requests, followers, and badges — plus the attached email and a
transfer checklist that makes the account exclusively yours.

## How long does it take?

Minutes from payment to credentials for standard tiers (via email and
Telegram). Premium tiers include a manual quality check, so expect
same-day delivery.

## How do I pay?

Cryptocurrency — Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, and other major coins — through an
encrypted checkout. Prices are in USD; the crypto amount converts at
checkout.

## Which tier do I need?

- Bots, CI, testing → **Fresh**
- Freelancing, job hunting → **Aged**
- Agencies, founders, max authority → **Veteran**
- Collecting, premium contracts → **Legend**

Full decision framework: [choosing-your-tier.md](choosing-your-tier.md).

## What are the most important badges?

**Arctic Code Vault** and **Mars 2020** — both frozen in time and impossible
to earn now. They prove the account's age better than anything else. Earned
badges like **Pull Shark**, **Starstruck**, **Galaxy Brain**, and **Pair
Extraordinaire** signal real contribution history.

## Is the transfer secure?

Yes, if you complete it. Change the password, swap in your email, enable
2FA, save recovery codes, and audit sessions, keys, and apps. Full playbook:
[transfer-security-playbook.md](transfer-security-playbook.md).

## What if something goes wrong?

The **replacement warranty** covers access issues within the warranty
period — free replacement with an equivalent or better profile. Support runs
24/7 on Telegram and WhatsApp.

## Can I get a specific account?

Yes — custom sourcing for creation years, follower counts, or badge
combinations is available. Ask before you buy.

## What's the biggest mistake buyers make?

Skipping the security lockdown after delivery. The account isn't yours until
you've changed the credentials, enabled 2FA, and cleared old access. See
[avoid-these-mistakes.md](avoid-these-mistakes.md) for the rest.
