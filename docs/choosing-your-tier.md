# Choosing Your Tier: A Buyer's Decision Framework

Picking between Fresh, Aged, Veteran, and Legend isn't about "bigger is
better" — it's about matching the account to your situation. Here's how to
think it through.

---

## Start with your audience

**Humans will judge this profile.** Recruiters, clients, investors,
maintainers — anyone who opens the profile page and forms an opinion in ten
seconds. For you, history depth and badges are the product. Lean toward Aged
or Veteran; go Legend if the stakes justify it.

**Only machines will use it.** CI runners, scrapers, bots, test automation.
Here, what matters is a working login with fewer rate limits and less spam
filtering. Fresh is usually enough; Aged if you want extra headroom.

**Both.** A freelancer who also runs automation on the same account needs
human credibility *and* machine headroom. That's squarely Aged-or-above
territory.

---

## Match the tier to the job

| Your situation | Recommended tier | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Bot / CI / testing account | Fresh | Cheapest working login; history irrelevant |
| Freelance bidding | Aged | Visible credibility without the premium price |
| Job hunting | Aged | History matches your résumé from day one |
| Client-facing agency dev | Veteran | Authority at a glance justifies higher rates |
| Fundraising founder | Veteran | Serious technical signal for due diligence |
| Premium contracts | Veteran / Legend | Maximum trust for maximum rates |
| Badge collecting | Legend | Frozen badges only exist here |

---

## The badge question

Ask yourself: do badges change your outcome? For a freelancer, an Arctic
Code Vault badge on the profile can genuinely shift a client's perception.
For a bot account, badges are decoration. If badges matter, you need Veteran
or Legend — the time-locked badges (Arctic, Mars 2020) physically cannot
exist on younger accounts, so don't expect them at lower tiers.

## Budget reality check

Each tier up is a real price jump, because each tier up represents years of
irreplaceable history. Set your ceiling *before* browsing. The most common
buyer's regret isn't overpaying — it's stretching for Legend when Aged would
have done the job, or cheaping out on Fresh when clients needed to see
history.

## When to ask for custom sourcing

The listed inventory covers the common cases. Go custom when you need:

- A specific **creation year** (e.g., pre-2020 for Arctic eligibility)
- A minimum **follower count**
- An exact **badge combination**
- A particular **username style**
